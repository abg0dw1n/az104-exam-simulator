Exam Simulator

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Exam Simulator is a private, invite-only practice site for IT certification exams, run by Andrew Godwin. This page explains what data the site uses and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address, used to sign you in. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your email address and basic profile (such as your name) from that provider. We don't receive your password.
  • Your practice progress: answers, flagged questions, exam scores and settings, so your progress follows you between devices.

How it's used

Only to let you sign in and to save and sync your progress. We don't sell your data, show ads, use it for marketing, or share it with anyone except the services that run the site.

Where it's stored

Sign-in and progress are handled by Supabase (hosted in the United States). The website is hosted by Vercel. Sign-in emails are sent through Gmail. Your progress is also kept in your browser's local storage so the site works offline; signing out removes it from that browser.

Who can see it

Your progress is protected so that only you can read or change it. The site owner can see the list of invited email addresses.

Deleting your data

To have your account and progress deleted, email andrewbgodwin@gmail.com. It will be removed within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
